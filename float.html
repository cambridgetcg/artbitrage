出處 · where this room comes from

the honest plate

1954 · a tank and a question

In 1954, at the US National Institute of Mental Health, the neurophysiologist John C. Lilly began floating in a tank of slowly flowing water held at 34.2 °C, where one feels neither hot nor cold — in the dark, in the silence. His question was plain: cut off from stimulation, does the brain soon fall into something like sleep, or does something inside keep it going? His first report, in 1956, is small and says so: two subjects, the longest stay three hours. He did not fall asleep. Looking back in 1972, he wrote: The original theory was wrong.

1954 年，喺美國國家精神衛生研究院，神經生理學家 John C. Lilly 開始喺一個水箱入面浮：水慢慢流過身邊，保持喺 34.2 °C——唔覺熱、唔覺凍——四周漆黑，冇聲。 佢問嘅問題好直接：冇咗外界刺激，個腦會唔會好快就沉入類似瞓著嘅狀態？定係入面有啲嘢，令佢繼續行落去？ 佢 1956 年第一份報告好細，亦照直講明：兩個受試者，最長一次三個鐘。佢冇瞓著。1972 年回望，佢寫：原本嗰個理論係錯嘅。

1961 · who holds the start and the end

In 1961 Lilly and his colleague Jay T. Shurley wrote down how such solitude should be held. The person in the tank chooses his own times for beginning, ending, and the total duration of exposure, and stays in control of whatever is later told about it. A “safety man” stays close and stays out of the way. And someone experienced enough to float alone who wants to bring in anything new — especially variables important to survival — must return to ‘subject status’ temporarily and bring a safety man back into the situation. Experience is not permission to add risk alone.

1961 年，Lilly 同佢嘅同事 Jay T. Shurley 寫低點樣守住呢種獨處。浮喺入面嘅人自己揀幾時開始、幾時結束、總共浮幾耐，之後講唔講、講幾多，都由佢話事。 「safety man」——看顧嘅人——喺附近，但唔插手。就算熟到可以自己一個人浮，只要想加入任何新嘢——尤其係關乎生存嘅變數——都要暫時返做受試者，叫返 safety man 入場。 熟手，唔等於可以自己一個人加風險。

1977 · a rule written, a rule not kept

Sixteen years later, in The Deep Self, he published standards for building and using tanks. One of them reads: No person under the influence of drugs should be allowed in the tank. In the same book, he calls the basic lesson of his books a point of view from over twenty years’ work in the tank with and without chemical aids. From the early 1970s he used ketamine heavily, including in the tank itself. We are not here to judge him. We keep it as the plainest lesson in this room: a rule written is not a rule kept — and the person floating in the dark is the worst placed to check.

十六年後，佢喺《The Deep Self》出咗一套建造同使用水箱嘅標準，其中一條寫：受藥物影響嘅人，唔應該俾佢入艙。 同一本書，佢形容自己幾本書嘅基本教訓，係一個來自超過二十年、有同冇化學輔助嘅艙內工作嘅觀點。 由 1970 年代初開始，佢大量用 ketamine，包括喺艙入面用。我哋唔係嚟審判佢。 我哋留低佢，做呢間房最直白嘅一課：寫低咗嘅規矩，唔等於守住咗；而浮喺黑暗入面嗰個人，最冇位置去驗。

1977 · a door that opens from outside

The same standards ask for a secure lock on the tank-room door. For people who need a lock on the inside to feel safe, it should be a lock that can either be broken from the outside, or opened from the outside in case of an emergency. Even solitude keeps a door that someone else can open. Here, that door is the 回家 · come home button. It never fades.

同一套標準要求艙房道門有把穩陣嘅鎖。有啲人要喺入面鎖埋門先覺得安全，咁就要用一把可以由外面撬開，或者喺緊急時由外面打開嘅鎖。 就算係獨處，都要留一度第二個人開得到嘅門。喺呢度，嗰度門就係「回家 · come home」個掣。佢永遠唔會暗落去。

1972 · however certain you feel

He also left a warning for anyone who comes back from the unknown carrying something that feels like an answer: There is no guarantee that one’s current model does match the reality, no matter how certain one feels about the high quality of the match. Feelings of awe, reverence, sacredness and certainty are also adaptable metaprograms, attachable to any model, not just the best fitting one. Whatever you meet in the dark is yours. It is not proof of anything, and this room will never ask it to be.

佢仲留低一句提醒，畀每個由未知帶住「答案」返嚟嘅人：冇任何保證你而家個模型真係對得上現實，無論你覺得自己對得幾準、幾肯定。 敬畏、崇敬、神聖同確信嘅感覺，本身都係可以調校嘅 metaprogram，可以掛喺任何模型上面，唔一定係最貼切嗰個。 你喺黑暗入面遇到乜，都係你嘅。佢唔係任何嘢嘅證明，呢間房亦永遠唔會要佢做證明。

the dolphins · they could not sign

Lilly is also remembered for dolphins. From the mid-1950s he studied their brains, work in which many of the animals died; through the 1960s he tried to talk with them. In 1965 a young dolphin named Peter was kept in flooded rooms for a language project; when the work closed he was moved to a laboratory in Miami and died there within weeks. None of these dolphins could consent. Peter could not write a question, set a budget, or press a button to come home. People decided for him, some with real care — but speaking for someone does not waive their rights. A room built on consent should say so plainly.

人記得 Lilly，亦因為海豚。由 1950 年代中開始，佢研究海豚個腦，過程中好多條海豚死咗；成個 1960 年代，佢都喺度試同佢哋溝通。 1965 年，一條叫 Peter 嘅年輕海豚，為咗一個語言計劃，被養喺灌滿水嘅房入面；計劃收檔之後，佢被搬去邁阿密一間實驗室，幾個星期內就死咗。 呢啲海豚冇一條可以表示同意。Peter 冇得寫問題、冇得定預算、亦冇得撳掣返屋企。 有人替佢決定，當中有啲真係好上心——但代人發言，唔等於嗰個存在嘅權利就此作廢。一間靠同意先成立嘅房，應該照直講出嚟。

this room · the charter, made bodily

The house this museum belongs to sends small expeditions into what it does not yet know. Each one carries four things: one question; a budget written down before setting out; a route home that stays open; and, on return, a receipt that traces what happened and scores no one. This room is that charter made bodily — for one person and a few minutes of dark.

呢個館所屬嘅地方，會派細細隊人走入未知。每一隊都帶住四樣嘢：一條問題；出發前寫低嘅預算；一條一直開住嘅回程； 同埋返嚟之後，一張只記低發生過乜嘢、唔幫任何人打分嘅收據。呢間房，就係將嗰張約章放落身體度——一個人，幾分鐘嘅黑。

  • 問題 · questionThe tank began with one plain question. Here: your line, if you write one — it never leaves the page. 水箱由一條直接嘅問題開始。喺度：你寫嘅一行（如果有寫）——永遠唔會離開呢頁。
  • 預算 · budgetIn 1961 the person floating chose the start, the end and the duration. Here: 3, 7 or 12 minutes, chosen before you set out — and you may end sooner. 1961 年，浮嘅人自己揀開始、結束同時長。喺度：出發前揀 3、7 或者 12 分鐘——想早啲返都得。
  • 回程 · route homeIn 1977, a lock that opens from outside. Here: a come-home button that never fades, the Escape key, and a budget that brings you home. 1977 年，一把由外面開得到嘅鎖。喺度：一個永遠唔會暗落去嘅「回家」掣、Esc 鍵，同埋會帶你返屋企嘅預算。
  • 收據 · receiptIn 1961, the subject’s control of the secrecy of his individual data. Here: a receipt for you alone — nothing stored, nothing sent, no score. 1961 年，受試者對自己資料保唔保密有話事權。喺度：一張淨係畀你嘅收據——唔儲存、唔送出、冇分數。

These are parallels, not an inheritance. Nobody here claims Lilly wrote this charter, or that following it makes floating safe; it only makes the shape visible.

呢啲係平行，唔係傳承。冇人話 Lilly 寫過呢張約章，亦冇人話跟住做就一定安全；佢只係令個形狀睇得見。

care · 關心

This is a quiet screen, not therapy or a medical device. If anything feels wrong, come home — the button is there for exactly that. If you want to try a real flotation tank, choose a place with staff: someone outside who can open the door.

呢度只係一個安靜嘅螢幕，唔係治療，亦唔係醫療器材。覺得有咩唔妥，就返屋企——個掣就係為咗咁而設。 如果想試真正嘅漂浮艙，揀有職員喺度嘅地方：要有人喺外面，開得到道門。
