The gallery can move with you

Between works.

What would you hang beside what? Bring two works together, let their images overlap, and see what one helps you notice in the other.

Follow the current

White water bends both scenes into motion. Follow it through the wave’s blue curl, then down past the mill’s red wheel.

What route does your eye take along the water—and where does it stop?

Near enough to enter

A dark branch interrupts the garden. A seated figure gathers out of loose strokes and leaves. Each gives you a different place to stand.

Follow the dark trunk, then the blue sleeve. Where are you drawn closer?

Held by the light

Light traces the jar’s rounded surface; a small golden glow steadies the hazy harbour. Let each bright place draw you in.

Where does light describe an edge, and where does it let an edge disappear?

These are invitations to look. Your connection may be entirely different.
